IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS

1

PATIENT INTAKE & PREPROCESSING

Intake

Patient arrives at the kiosk, selects language (Hindi/English), and enters ABHA ID or phone number. A consent-first DPDP 2023 framework gates the session before clinical intake starts.

2

MULTIMODAL SPEECH & OCR CAPTURE

Multimodal

Patients articulate symptoms using Sarvam AI ASR or high-contrast touch cards. Physical past prescriptions are scanned via Document OCR to automatically digitize prior medications, dosages, and history.

3

DUAL-STREAM AI TRIAGE & RED-FLAGS

AI Triage

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash structures raw facts into Allopathic HPI and 10-point AYUSH Dashavidha Pariksha (Prakriti, Agni, Koshtha). The deterministic engine instantly surfaces acute red flags.

4

DOCTOR VERIFICATION (HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP)

HITL

Prioritized patient cards stream into the Doctor OPD Dashboard via Supabase Realtime WebSockets. Clinicians inspect clear provenance badges (Patient/OCR/Doctor) and verify or modify facts inline.

5

FHIR STANDARDS & EMR HANDOFF

Export

Validated clinical encounters export as standard HL7 FHIR R4 JSON Bundles and printable bilingual clinical summaries, enabling zero-friction integration into hospital EMR systems and national ABDM registers.
